What SiteFix AI checks

Four categories, one severity scale: critical, warning, and passed.

SEO

Search visibility

  • Title tags, meta descriptions and heading structure
  • Indexability, robots rules and canonical tags
  • Structured data and Open Graph metadata
Performance

Speed & technical health

  • HTTPS, redirects and server response time
  • Page weight and load-time signals
  • Technical errors that quietly hurt rankings
UX

Usability & accessibility

  • Mobile viewport and responsive setup
  • Image alt text and form field labels
  • Navigation clarity and heading structure
Conversion

Turning visits into action

  • Call-to-action presence and clarity
  • Contact forms and reachability
  • Content depth and completeness
